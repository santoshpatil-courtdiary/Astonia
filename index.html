Bye-laws Governing Levy and Recovery of Maintenance
Extracted from the Model Bye-laws for a Co-operative Housing Society, 2014, adopted by the Commissioner for Co-operation and Registrar, Maharashtra State, and ordinarily adopted with or without modification by individual societies including this society. The bye-law numbers correspond to the 2014 model; a society registered under an earlier model should cross-check against its own registered bye-laws, since numbering differs across the 1985 and 2014 models.
Bye-lawSubjectSubstance, in brief
66Funds of the societySets out the various funds to be maintained, including the repairs and maintenance fund, the sinking fund, and the major repairs fund.
68Sinking fundThe society shall collect a sinking fund at a rate not less than 0.25 per cent per annum of the construction cost of each flat, to be used only for structural additions, repairs or reconstruction.
69Repair and maintenance fundTo be collected at a minimum of 0.75 per cent per annum of the construction cost, subject to a minimum decided by the general body, for day to day repairs and maintenance.
70Apportionment of outgoings among membersPrescribes the components of the monthly contribution, being service charges, municipal taxes, water charges, expenses on repairs and maintenance, contribution to the sinking fund, insurance premium, lease rent if any, non-occupancy charges, parking charges, education fund contribution to the federal society, and any other charge approved by the general body, and the manner of apportioning each component, whether equally, by area, or by number of members, as resolved by the general body.
72Non-occupancy chargesChargeable where a member permits use of the flat by a person other than a member of his family, at a rate not exceeding the ceiling prescribed from time to time by government circular, presently ten per cent of the service charges excluding municipal taxes, as per the circular dated 1 August 2001.
73Determination of contributionThe Managing Committee shall determine the amount of contribution payable by each member every year, or as often as required, based on the budget approved by the general body.
75Payment of the contributionEvery member shall pay his contribution towards outgoings within the fifteenth day of the commencement of the period for which it is payable, or such other date as the Managing Committee may fix, failing which the amount is treated as an arrear.
76Society's lienThe society shall have a first and paramount lien on the shares, interest in capital, property and any other amount payable to a member towards any sum due from him to the society.
77Investment of surplus fundsGoverns deployment of the sinking fund and other funds in permitted securities.
79Notice of demandA written notice of demand is to be issued calling upon the defaulting member to pay the amount in arrears within the period specified in the notice.
154(b)(24)Interest on defaulted paymentsSimple interest shall be charged on the amount remaining unpaid beyond the due date, at a rate not exceeding twenty one per cent per annum, or such other rate as may be fixed by the general body from time to time by resolution.
154(b)(24)(i)Recovery through the Co-operative CourtOn persistent default, the Managing Committee may refer the dispute regarding recovery of dues to the Co-operative Court under section 91 of the Act, or may apply to the Registrar for a certificate under section 101 for recovery of dues as arrears of land revenue.
155Forfeiture and other action against defaultersProvides for restriction of a defaulting member's voting rights and other privileges of membership under section 27(4) of the Act until the arrears are cleared.
160Expulsion of a memberA member may, on grounds set out in the bye-law including persistent default in payment of dues, be expelled by a resolution passed by not less than three-fourths of the members present at a general body meeting called for the purpose, with the previous sanction of the Registrar.

The interest rate, the non-occupancy charge percentage and the sinking fund contribution used elsewhere in this application should mirror the figures actually adopted by this society's general body from time to time; the percentages above are the outer statutory or circular limits and not necessarily the rate in force here. The Maintenance Rate History, Sinking Fund History and Interest Rate History in the Bill Heads and Rates tab are the operative record for this society.

Recovery Procedure for Persistent Default - Step-wise Note for the Secretary
A working sequence commonly followed, to be read with the society's own registered bye-laws and with legal advice taken at the appropriate stage before initiating court or Co-operative Court proceedings.
StageActionTypical Timeline
1Monthly bill raised and due date communicated (bye-law 75)On or before the date fixed by the Managing Committee
2First reminder / notice of demand on default (bye-law 79)Promptly after the due date, once arrears appear on the register
3Interest begins to accrue on the unpaid amount (bye-law 154(b)(24))From the due date, at the rate resolved by the general body
4Second notice / reminder, restriction of voting rights considered (bye-law 155)Where arrears continue beyond a further month or two
5Final notice before reference to Co-operative CourtReasonable further period, commonly fifteen to thirty days
6Managing Committee resolution to refer the matter for recoveryRecorded in the minute book, authorising the Secretary to proceed
7Dispute filed before the Co-operative Court under section 91, or application to the Registrar under section 101 for a recovery certificateAs advised, having regard to the amount involved and the member's response
